I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at UC San Diego, where I am advised by Prof. Jorge Cortés and affiliated with the Contextual Robotics Institute. I received my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering, with Honors and a Minor in Computer Science, from IIT Bombay, where I worked with Prof. Vivek Borkar.

Most of my research is focused on Koopman operator theory: finding accurate linear representations of nonlinear dynamics. The Koopman operator lifts a nonlinear system into a space of observables where it evolves linearly, which opens the door to spectral analysis, prediction, and control with linear tools. The catch is that any practical approximation lives in a finite-dimensional subspace, and its quality depends entirely on how close that subspace is to invariant. Much of my work is about that question: how to measure invariance, and how to find subspaces that have it.

I started out in robust control of neural feedback loops, deriving stability certificates and controller synthesis methods for systems with neural networks in the loop. From there I moved to the Koopman approximation side, developing principal-vector methods that prune large dictionaries (including kernel dictionaries) down to accurate, nearly invariant subspaces.

More recently, I have turned these ideas toward generative modeling. Flow-based generative models transport noise to data along the trajectories of a learned dynamical system, and flow maps learn to jump directly between points on those trajectories, enabling generation in just a few steps. Because a flow map is itself the solution operator of a dynamical system, Koopman theory gives a natural way to structure it. Read more in Koopman Flow Maps.

My current research focuses on applying these Koopman-inspired architectures to flow language models and world models, where the same structure could make few-step generation of text and of future states more accurate and efficient. More broadly, I am interested in bringing tools from dynamical systems and control to problems in generative AI and robotics, where structure can make learned models more accurate, interpretable, and efficient.

Always happy to chat about Koopman operators, control, or generative modeling. Feel free to reach out via email!
